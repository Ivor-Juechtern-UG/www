We automate industrial processes using any IEC 61131-3-compatible industrial controller.

Our focus is on the management of energy systems.

We plan, implement and maintain energy systems in industry and in energy hubs. In these hubs, several independent energy producers and consumers join forces to represent both their individual and their shared interests and to optimise their energy supply.

We carry out our projects in Germany, France, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Austria.
