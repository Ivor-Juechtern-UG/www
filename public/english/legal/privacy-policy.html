In Brief

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising and no social media plugins. Fonts and images are served by this website itself; no content from third parties (for example Google) is loaded when you visit it.

Hosting

The website is hosted by statichost.eu, a European provider of static website hosting with servers in the European Union.

In order for a page to be displayed, your browser transmits technically necessary data to the server, in particular your IP address, the address requested, the date and time, and the browser identifier. This data is processed solely in order to deliver the website. According to statichost.eu, no personal data about visits to the website is stored in the process.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely and reliably.

Contact by E-mail or Telephone

If you contact us by e-mail or telephone, we process the information you provide (for example your name, e-mail address, telephone number and the content of your message) in order to respond to your enquiry.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract or to pre-contractual measures, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the data as soon as it is no longer required for handling your enquiry, provided that no statutory retention obligations prevent its deletion.

Your Rights
